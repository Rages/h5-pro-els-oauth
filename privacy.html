Privacy Policy — H5 Pro ELS Mini PC

Effective date: October 1, 2026

H5 Pro ELS Mini PC is a private, single-user utility operated by the owner for synchronizing NanoELS H5 firmware release artifacts between the owner's Mini PC and the owner's Google Drive.

Google Drive access

The utility accesses Google Drive files and folders only as required to perform owner-initiated or owner-scheduled synchronization. It processes file content and file metadata locally on the owner's Mini PC and transfers them to or from the owner's Google Drive.

Data use and sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for analytics. The utility has no public user registration and is not offered to other users.

Credentials and retention

OAuth credentials are stored only in a protected local configuration on the owner's Mini PC. Files remain in the locations selected by the owner and are retained according to the owner's local and Google Drive settings.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time in Google Account security settings or remove the local rclone remote configuration.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be submitted through the Rages GitHub profile.
